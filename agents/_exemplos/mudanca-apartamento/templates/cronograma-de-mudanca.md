# Moving schedule

Move on: DD/MM/YYYY (weekday) | From: neighborhood and city of origin | To: neighborhood and city of destination
Current home: rented, owned or a family member's | Volume: range | Help: who does what

Mark the 3 critical tasks with [!] and write the reason in one sentence.

## 8 to 5 weeks before (from DD/MM to DD/MM)

- [ ] Lock in the moving date and check the notice period in the contract (owner: you) [!]
- [ ] Sort what will be donated, sold or thrown away (owner: you)
- [ ] Ask for at least 3 quotes using the same written request (owner: you) [!]

## 4 to 3 weeks before (from DD/MM to DD/MM)

- [ ] Choose the moving company and confirm in writing (owner: you)
- [ ] Book the service elevator and the time slot in both buildings (owner: you) [!]
- [ ] Buy or borrow boxes, tape and packing paper (owner: you)

## 2 weeks before (from DD/MM to DD/MM)

- [ ] Pack what is not used day to day (owner: you or the company)
- [ ] Tell the bank, employer, school and health plan about the new address (owner: you)
- [ ] Request the connection of accounts at the new address and the closing at the old one (owner: you)

## 1 week before (from DD/MM to DD/MM)

- [ ] Empty and defrost the fridge
- [ ] Prepare the first-night box or suitcase (documents, medication, chargers, bed linen)
- [ ] Confirm the time and the person in charge with the moving company

## The day before and moving day (DD/MM)

- [ ] Take apart the agreed furniture and protect the floor at the entrance
- [ ] Follow the loading with the inventory in hand
- [ ] Read the electricity, water and gas meters and photograph each empty room
- [ ] Take a final walk through the cupboards and the laundry area

## Up to 2 weeks after (by DD/MM)

- [ ] Check that the address notices were accepted and that mail is arriving
- [ ] Check the first bill of the accounts at the new address
- [ ] Keep photos, protocol numbers and proof of the move and of the handover of the keys

Deadlines of agencies, banks and utility companies vary: check each one on its official website.
