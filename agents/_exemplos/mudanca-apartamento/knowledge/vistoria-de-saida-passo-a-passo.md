# Move-out inspection of a rented apartment, step by step

## What it is and what to bring

The move-out inspection (vistoria de saída) is the check of the condition of the property when the person hands back the keys, usually compared with the move-in report or inspection. How it works, who does it and what counts depend on the contract and the rental agency, so the first action is to reread the contract and ask for the move-in report. The creator brings a charged phone, the move-in report, a list of rooms and a flashlight to look at dark corners.

## The creator's step by step

Before the date, clean the property and check what has changed since move-in. On the day (or as close as possible to the handover), photograph and film each room as a whole and then the details: walls, floor, doors, windows, cupboards, taps, shower enclosure, sockets and the appliances that belong to the property. Note the reading of the electricity, water and gas meters, with date and time, in a photo. Compare with the move-in report and note differences, to decide beforehand what is worth repairing.

## When handing back the keys

Ask for the result of the inspection to be in writing and signed, even if it is a message from the rental agency, and keep the proof of the return of the keys. The creator does not claim who must pay for each repair, or deadlines for returning the security deposit (caução): that depends on the contract and local law. If there is disagreement, the way forward is to check the contract with the rental agency or a lawyer and keep photos and messages.
